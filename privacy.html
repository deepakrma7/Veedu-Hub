Privacy Policy

Veedu Hub · Effective date: 3 October 2026 · Last updated: 3 October 2026

This policy explains how Veedu Hub ("we", "us") collects, uses, shares and protects your personal information when you respond to our advertisements on Facebook, Instagram or other Meta platforms, fill in our enquiry form, or visit our website. We handle personal data in line with applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

In short: we ask for only the details needed to answer your property enquiry, we use them to contact you about that enquiry, we do not sell your data, and you can ask us to correct or delete it at any time.

1. Who we are

3. How we use your information

  • To contact you about the property you enquired about, by phone or email.
  • To schedule and confirm site visits.
  • To reply in Tamil or English.
  • To share related project updates or offers, only where you have agreed.
  • To improve our ads and services, using summary or anonymised statistics.
  • To meet legal, tax and regulatory duties, and to prevent fraud or misuse.

4. Legal basis and consent

We process your data on the basis of your consent, given when you submit a form or message us, and for limited legitimate uses allowed by law. You can withdraw consent at any time (section 9). Withdrawal does not affect what we did before you withdrew.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with:

  • Project owners or developers of the property you enquired about, so they can arrange visits and answer your questions.
  • Service providers that help us run our business, such as Meta (Facebook, Instagram), CRM or spreadsheet tools, calling and messaging services, and cloud storage. They may use the data only for the services they provide to us.
  • Authorities where the law requires it.

Meta processes data collected through its platforms under its own privacy policy, which you can read at facebook.com/privacy/policy.

6. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for up to 12 months after your last interaction, or longer where the law requires or where a purchase or booking takes place. After that we delete or anonymise them.

7. Cookies and the Meta Pixel

This website does not use cookies, analytics or the Meta Pixel. Our Facebook and Instagram ads are delivered by Meta, which may use its own tools under its own policy. You can manage ad preferences in your Facebook or Instagram settings.

8. Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including password-protected accounts, limited staff access and secure cloud tools. No online system is completely secure. If a breach affecting your data occurs, we will act as required by law.

9. Your rights

You may ask us to: access the data we hold about you; correct or update it; delete it; stop contacting you; or withdraw consent. You may also nominate another person to exercise your rights if you cannot. To do so, write to deepak.dhanusu@protonmail.com. We aim to reply within 30 days.

You can also opt out of messages by replying STOP.

10. Children

Our services are for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you think a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Links to other sites

Our ads or pages may link to other websites, such as Meta or a developer's site. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

12. Grievance officer

If you are not satisfied with our reply, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India once it is operational, or to the relevant authority under applicable law.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The latest version and the date above will always be shown on this page.
